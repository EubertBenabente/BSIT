About Us

Who We Are

The BSIT program at Cebu Technological University - Argao Campus is dedicated to producing competent IT professionals equipped with technical expertise, ethics, and leadership in the technology field.

Mission

CTU as a premier, inclusive, globally recognized research and innovation, smart, community-responsive, and sustainable technological university.

Vision

The University shall primarily provide leading-edge degree programs, innovative professional, entrepreneurial, and technical instruction as well as research, extension and resource generation programs that address both needs of the region and the nation in the context of the global knowledge economy, Fifth Industrial Revolution, and Sustainability.
